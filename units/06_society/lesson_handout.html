DSE Computational Physics — Lesson Handout

Unit 06: Physics and Society 物理與社會 — S5

Learning Outcomes 學習目標

  • Radioactive decay 放射性衰變: random nature; activity A ∝ N; half-life from graph.
  • Radiation types 輻射種類: alpha/beta/gamma penetrating & ionising power.
  • Chain reaction 鏈式反應: neutron multiplication factor k; critical mass.
  • Mass–energy 質能關係: ΔE = Δmc2; electron-volt & atomic mass unit.
  • Energy sources 能源: solar P = S ⋅ A ⋅ η; wind $P = \tfrac{1}{2}\eta\rho Av^3$.
  • Radioisotope uses 放射性同位素應用: radiotherapy, tracers, imaging.

Key Formulas 核心公式

Formula Meaning
N = N0 2−t/T decay curve (half-life form)
p = 1 − e−λdt decay probability (Monte Carlo)
ΔE = Δm c2 mass–energy (1 amu ≈ 931.5 MeV)
P = S ⋅ A ⋅ η solar power
$P = \tfrac{1}{2}\eta\rho\pi r^2 v^3$ wind power
A = λN activity
